# 🎯 Sales Playbook: Software Asset Purchase & Services Agreement

## 📌 Master Agreement Summary
* **Seller:** Yorkstead Systems (Colorado Sole Proprietorship / LLC)
* **Buyer:** Denver Express Warehousing & Cross-Docking / No Limit Trucking LLC
* **Transaction Type:** Custom Software Capital Asset Purchase & Deployment Agreement
* **Total Software Purchase Price:** $9,500.00 USD

---

## 📜 Key Contract Clauses & Covenants

### 1. Intellectual Property & Code Ownership
Upon receipt of the final milestone payment, Seller grants Buyer perpetual, exclusive, fully-paid, irrevocable rights to all customized source code, database schemas, styling, and application logic comprising the ReworkFlow Denver Express installation.

### 2. Territorial Exclusivity Covenant
Seller agrees not to sell, license, or deploy the ReworkFlow platform to any direct warehousing, cold-storage, or cross-dock competitor operating within a **15-mile geographical radius** of 6030 Washington St, Denver, CO 80216 for a period of twenty-four (24) months.

### 3. Payment Milestones
* **Milestone 1 (50% — $4,750.00):** Due upon signing of this Agreement.
* **Milestone 2 (30% — $2,850.00):** Due upon delivery and staging verification of the operational routes.
* **Milestone 3 (20% — $1,900.00):** Due upon live dock walkthrough, first truck processing, and final handoff.

### 4. Hardware Sourcing & Provisioning (Optional Add-On)
Buyer may elect to procure turnkey hardware from Seller:
* **Package A (2-Bay):** $980.00 (2x HOTWAV R9 Pro Rugged Tablets + RAM Mounts).
* **Package B (6-Bay):** $2,850.00 (6x Tablets + RAM Mounts).

### 5. Ongoing Support & Maintenance (Optional SLA)
Buyer may elect an ongoing operational SLA at **$450.00/month** (includes server maintenance, database backups, uptime monitoring, and 4-hour critical issue response), or may take full custody of the codebase for maintenance by their designated agency (**SpinFlow.ai**) with zero monthly fee.
