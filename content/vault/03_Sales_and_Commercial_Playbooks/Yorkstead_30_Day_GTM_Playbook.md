# 🎯 Sales Playbook: Yorkstead 30-Day Marketing & Sales Launch

## 📌 Strategic Overview
A focused 4-week execution sprint designed to close the Denver Express deal, establish a localized reference hub along the I-25/I-70 industrial corridor, and validate the $9,500 capital asset sales engine.

---

## 🗓️ Weekly Execution Cadence

### Week 1: Groundwork & Denver Express Closing
* **Day 1–2:** Execute initial 45-second phone outreach to Steve Chapman and Dale Burget.
* **Day 3:** Conduct on-site dock walkthrough and live 3-minute dual-device demonstration at 6030 Washington St.
* **Day 4:** Deliver formal proposal and `Denver_Express_ReworkFlow_Master_Brief.pdf`.
* **Day 5:** Sign agreement and collect Milestone 1 deposit ($4,750.00).

### Week 2: Staging & Hardware Assembly
* **Day 6–8:** Procure HOTWAV R9 Pro tablets and RAM Mounts Tough-Claw hardware.
* **Day 9–10:** Configure custom domain (`rework.denverexpressco.com`), kiosk browser settings, and test staging routes.

### Week 3: Dock Deployment & Live Pilot
* **Day 11–13:** Mount hardware in Bay 1 & Bay 2 on Toyota forklifts; train Craig and dock operators (20-minute onboarding).
* **Day 14–15:** Process first 3 live shifted-freight loads; verify Evidence Certificate generation and QuickBooks export.

### Week 4: Case Study & Regional Expansion
* **Day 16–20:** Document time savings and collected revenue figures from Denver Express live operations.
* **Day 21–25:** Launch outreach to secondary cross-docks and cold storage facilities outside the 15-mile exclusivity zone (Commerce City, Henderson, Aurora, Greely).
* **Day 26–30:** Review metrics, refine pitch, and scale pipeline.
