# 🎯 Sales Playbook: Warm Referral Pitch — Morelli Playbook

## 📌 Target
* **Contacts:** Steve Chapman or Dale Burget (Owners, Denver Express & No Limit Trucking)
* **Phone Goal:** Secure a 30-minute on-site dock walkthrough at 6030 Washington St.

---

## 📞 45-Second Cold Call Phone Script

> **"Hey Steve, this is [Name] with Yorkstead Systems. I spent years in manufacturing operations right down the road at Elward Systems with the Morelli brothers.**
> 
> **I’m calling because I know you guys get hammered at Washington Street with trucks coming down Floyd Hill on I-70 that have shifted loads, broken pallets, and drivers who are out of driving hours.**
> 
> **Right now, if a driver searches for emergency rework at 4 PM, they either get your voicemail or call three other docks while you lose an $800 job.**
> 
> **We built a simple 4-step mobile system called ReworkFlow that lets stranded drivers lock in a dock bay from their phone in 30 seconds, lets your forklift operators take before-and-after photos in under two minutes, and sends a signed proof certificate straight to your billing office.**
> 
> **I’m not selling monthly software subscriptions. We sell custom shopfloor tools you own 100% as a business asset. I’d like to stop by Washington Street for 20 minutes this Thursday, show you the mobile screen on a tablet, and see if it makes sense for your bays. Do mornings or afternoons work better for you?"**

---

## 🛡️ Objection Handling Guide

### Objection 1: *"We already use QuickBooks and paper clipboards. It works fine."*
* **Response:** *"Paper works great until a broker disputes an $800 pallet breakdown charge three weeks later and you can’t find the greasy paper ticket. ReworkFlow doesn't replace QuickBooks — it actually creates the invoice CSV with before-and-after photos and the driver's signature attached. That means zero chargebacks and zero manual data entry for your office."*

### Objection 2: *"My forklift drivers are old-school. They won't touch computers."*
* **Response:** *"That's exactly why we didn't build an 'app'. There is nothing to download and no passwords. The tablet is bolted to the forklift cage with giant touch buttons you can tap with work gloves on. All the driver does is tap 'Bay 2', snap two photos of the freight, and hand it to the trucker to sign with their finger. It takes 90 seconds."*

### Objection 3: *"We already have a web marketing agency (SpinFlow.ai)."*
* **Response:** *"SpinFlow does great marketing and web design. We don't do marketing. We build industrial operations tools. In fact, our code is built so cleanly in Next.js that we can hand the entire platform over to SpinFlow to host directly on `rework.denverexpressco.com`. You get the best of both worlds."*

### Objection 4: *"How much does this cost?"*
* **Response:** *"It's a flat $9,500 capital asset purchase. You own the code 100%. No monthly per-user fees, no recurring license trap. At two extra rework trucks a week, it pays for itself in under 58 days."*
