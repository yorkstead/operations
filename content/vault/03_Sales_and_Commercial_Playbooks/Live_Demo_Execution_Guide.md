# 🎯 Sales Playbook: Live Demo Execution Guide

## 📌 Objective
Execute an airtight, high-impact **3-minute dual-device demonstration** that visually proves how ReworkFlow coordinates the driver on the highway, the forklift operator on the dock, and the dispatcher in the front office in real-time.

---

## 📱 Hardware Setup (Dual-Device)
1. **Device 1 (Your Smartphone / Tablet):** Open [rework.yorkstead.com/dock](https://rework.yorkstead.com/dock) (representing the forklift terminal).
2. **Device 2 (Your Laptop):** Open [rework.yorkstead.com/office](https://rework.yorkstead.com/office) (representing the dispatcher's desk). Set laptop volume to 75% to ensure the Web Audio alert chime is audible.

---

## ⏱️ 3-Minute Demonstration Script

* **Minute 1: The Highway Reservation (`/reserve` or `/maps`)**
  * Show the laptop or open `/reserve` on the phone.
  * *"Imagine a reefer driver coming down I-70 through the Eisenhower Tunnel. 4 pallets shifted. His ELD clock has 35 minutes left. He searches on Google, finds Denver Express, taps his problem, and sees an instant $550 estimate. He taps 'Reserve Bay 2' — and boom, a 45-minute countdown clock starts."*

* **Minute 2: The Forklift Intake (`/dock`)**
  * Pick up the phone with the oversized `/dock` terminal.
  * *"The truck pulls into Washington St Bay 2. Your forklift operator doesn't scramble for a clipboard. He taps 'Bay 2 Intake'. He taps 'Take Photo' — snaps the messy pallet. He counts '3 pallets restacked, 1 roll wrap'. He hands the tablet out the cab window, the driver signs with his work glove, and taps 'Complete'."*

* **Minute 3: The Front Office Payoff (`/office`)**
  * Immediately point to the laptop screen.
  * A clear, synthesized chime rings out.
  * Bay 2 turns **Green: Completed**.
  * Click **"View Certificate PDF"**: Show the formatted Evidence Certificate with timestamps, photos, and signature.
  * Click **"Export to QuickBooks"**: Show the instantaneous CSV generation.
  * *"Your office didn't touch paper. The broker has zero basis to dispute the invoice. You get paid in 48 hours instead of 45 days."*
