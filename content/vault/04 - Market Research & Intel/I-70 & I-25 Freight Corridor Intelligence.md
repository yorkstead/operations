# 🚚 Market Intelligence: The I-70 / I-25 Freight Rework Corridor

## 🏔️ The Physical Geography of Load Shifts

Every week, hundreds of over-the-road (OTR) semi-trucks haul 40,000 to 45,000 lbs of consumer freight eastbound across the Rocky Mountains on Interstate 70:
1. **The Downgrade:** Trucks descend through Eisenhower Tunnel, Vail Pass, and the 6% downgrade of **Floyd Hill into Golden**.
2. **The Damage:** Extreme braking, switchbacks, and sudden deceleration cause pallets of bottled beverages, canned goods, and packaging materials to lean, tip, or burst shrink wrap.
3. **The Rejection:** Receiving distribution centers in Commerce City, Aurora, and Henderson (King Soopers/Kroger, Walmart, Sysco, UNFI) immediately reject trailers with tipped pallets.
4. **The Port of Entry:** Shifting weight puts tractor tandem or trailer axles over legal Colorado DOT bridge limits.

---

## ⏳ The Three Pressures on Stranded Truckers

1. **The ELD Clock:** Drivers have strict Federal Hours of Service limits. Often they have under 45 minutes of legal drive time remaining before forced shutdown.
2. **Broker Authorization Deadlock:** Drivers cannot pay out-of-pocket cash ($400–$700). Brokers require a firm price upfront before issuing an electronic **Comchek** or **EFS** code.
3. **The Phone Tag Void:** Drivers search *"cross dock near me"* on Google Maps, call facilities, and get voicemail or non-committal answers.
