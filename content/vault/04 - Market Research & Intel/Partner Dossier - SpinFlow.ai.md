# 🤝 Partner Dossier: SpinFlow.ai (Lingows)

> **Role for Client:** Web design, hosting, and marketing agency for Denver Express (`denverexpressco.com`).  
> **Corporate Identity:** SpinFlow / Lingows Media LLC.  
> **Tech Stack:** Modern React, TanStack, Tailwind CSS, high-performance static/SSR frontend hosting.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Relationship with Yorkstead

Rather than viewing SpinFlow as a competitor or pitching Denver Express on replacing them, Yorkstead positions SpinFlow as a vital strategic partner:

1. **Stack Compatibility:** ReworkFlow is built on **Next.js / React / Tailwind CSS**—the identical technology philosophy used by SpinFlow.
2. **Subdomain Integration:** ReworkFlow is designed to be mapped seamlessly to a subdomain of Denver Express's existing domain: `rework.denverexpressco.com`.
3. **The "Vendor Independence" Pitch:** During the sales conversation with Steve Chapman and Dale Burget, Yorkstead explains:
   > *"You don't have to stay married to Yorkstead for monthly maintenance. If you want SpinFlow to host and maintain ReworkFlow alongside your main website, we will conduct a 1-hour engineering handover session with their tech team and deliver all source code directly to your GitHub repository."*
4. **Win-Win Dynamic:** SpinFlow gains a happy client with expanded web functionality, Denver Express avoids software lock-in, and Yorkstead closes the \$9,500 buyout with zero pushback.
