> Sourced and validated for heavy industrial cross-dock operations at 6030 Washington St.  
> Eliminates fragile consumer iPads and avoids \$3,000 Honeywell/Zebra scanner markups.

## 📱 Primary Tablet: HOTWAV R9 Pro 11" Rugged Android 14

* **Price:** \$259.99 – \$289.99 (Amazon Prime)
* **Battery:** **20,080 mAh** monster capacity. Operates 3 to 5 continuous 8-hour shifts without recharging. Eliminates costly forklift DC-to-DC electrical converter wiring.
* **Environmental Rating:** **IP68 / IP69K Waterproof & Dustproof**. Resistant to high-pressure hot-water washdown, reefer melt, diesel exhaust, and freezing warehouse temperatures.
* **Display:** 11-inch 2K FHD+ screen with high-nit sunlight readability and capacitive **glove-touch mode**.
* **Connectivity:** **4G Dual SIM LTE + Dual-Band Wi-Fi**. Guarantees uninterrupted connectivity when forklift operators enter deep inside 53-foot metal dry vans where warehouse Wi-Fi drops.
* **Camera:** 64MP rear inspection camera with LED flash for documenting freight damage.

## 🔩 Mounting System: RAM MOUNTS Heavy Duty

* **Roll-Cage Clamp:** **RAM MOUNTS Tough-Claw™ Medium** (`RAP-B-404U`) (\$48.99)
  * Clamps securely onto 1" to 1.6" round, square, or odd-shaped roll-cage overhead guard bars.
  * Installed in 60 seconds with zero drilling or permanent forklift modification.
* **Tablet Cradle:** **RAM MOUNTS X-Grip® Spring-Loaded 12" Cradle** (`RAM-HOL-UN11U`) (\$121.99)
  * Spring-loaded rubber-tipped posts expand and lock around the 11" HOTWAV chassis.
  * Patented rubber ball-and-socket design dampens severe shock vibrations from driving over steel dock levelers and cracked concrete.
