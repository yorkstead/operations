## ⚙️ Operational Pain Points Being Solved

1. **Highway Search Vacuum:** Drivers search Google Maps on I-70 after shifting cargo on Floyd Hill. Denver Express currently has no instant booking, causing drivers to bounce to competitors.
2. **ELD Clock Pressure:** Drivers have <45 minutes before hours-of-service violations. ReworkFlow's 45-minute bay hold solves this immediately.
3. **Broker Comchek Delays:** Upfront transparent estimates let drivers get Comchek / EFS advances authorized before pulling off the highway.
4. **Dock-to-Desk Disconnect:** The front office currently uses paper and foot traffic. `/office` provides real-time Web Audio chimes and live bay status.
5. **Post-Dispatch Chargebacks:** Forklift operators take watermarked GPS/timestamped before/after photos and capture digital driver signatures on the [[Industrial Hardware Specification|HOTWAV R9 Pro]] tablet, creating an immutable PDF evidence certificate and QuickBooks export.
