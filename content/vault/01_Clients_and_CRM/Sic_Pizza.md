# 🏢 Client Dossier: Sic Pizza

## 📌 Executive Summary
Sic Pizza is an independent high-volume artisan pizzeria in the Denver metro area. They required a streamlined digital ordering and kitchen display system (KDS) that eliminated third-party marketplace fees (DoorDash/UberEats 30% cuts) while providing rock-solid ticket coordination between the front counter, kitchen line, and dispatch.

* **Target System:** Sic Pizza Order & Kitchen OS
* **Stack:** Next.js 15, Drizzle ORM, SQLite/PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS, Web Audio kitchen chimes.
* **Deployment:** Live on Vercel with real-time ticket state progression (`New` -> `Baking` -> `Ready` -> `Out for Delivery`).

* **Kitchen Display System (KDS):** High-contrast, large-button touchscreen UI designed for high-heat, flour-dusted environments.
