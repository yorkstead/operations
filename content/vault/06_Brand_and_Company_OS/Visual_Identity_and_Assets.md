# 🏛️ Brand & Company OS: Visual Identity & Design Standards

## 🎨 Color Palette
* **Industrial Obsidian:** `#0f172a` (Slate 900) — Primary UI backgrounds and dark panels.
* **Terminal Zinc:** `#18181b` (Zinc 900) — Surface cards and table headers.
* **Safety Emerald / Active Green:** `#10b981` (Emerald 500) — Live bay indicators, completed actions.
* **Warning Amber:** `#f59e0b` (Amber 500) — Pending reservations, 45-minute countdown timers.
* **Emergency Vermillion:** `#ef4444` (Red 500) — Shifted load alerts, critical errors.
* **Clean White / Text:** `#f8fafc` (Slate 50) — High-contrast typography readable in direct daylight.

---

## 🔤 Typography Standards
* **Display & Headings:** `Inter` or `Geist Sans` — Clean, modern, highly legible.
* **Data, Terminal & Monospace:** `JetBrains Mono` or `Geist Mono` — For timestamps, pallet IDs, VINs, and part numbers.
