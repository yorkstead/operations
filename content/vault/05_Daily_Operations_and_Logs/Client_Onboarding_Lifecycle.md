# 📅 Operations Manual: Turnkey 14-Day Client Onboarding Lifecycle

```
Day 1–3: Scoping & Agreement
├── 30-minute on-site dock walkthrough
├── Agreement execution & Milestone 1 deposit ($4,750)
└── Facility parameter intake (number of bays, hourly labor rate, wrap prices)

Day 4–7: Customization & Staging
├── Subdomain routing (e.g. rework.denverexpressco.com)
├── Pricing matrix configuration in ReworkFlow rate engine
└── Hardware bundle ordering & procurement

Day 8–10: Hardware Assembly & Bench Testing
├── Tablet unboxing, screen protector installation, and kiosk profile provisioning
├── Mounting bench test with RAM Tough-Claw assemblies
└── Milestone 2 demonstration & approval ($2,850)

Day 11–14: Dock Go-Live & Operator Training
├── Physical mounting on forklifts in Bay 1 & Bay 2
├── 20-minute dock operator training session with shift superintendent
├── Live processing of first inbound freight load
└── Milestone 3 completion & final sign-off ($1,900)
```
