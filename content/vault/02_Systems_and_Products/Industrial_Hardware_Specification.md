# 🛠️ Hardware Blueprint: Industrial Forklift & Dock Terminal

## 📌 Overview
Software fails on industrial docks when run on consumer hardware. Consumer iPads crack, overheat, freeze in reefer lockers, and have batteries that die midway through a 10-hour shift.

Yorkstead has standardized on a field-tested, rugged hardware bundle designed specifically for Toyota, Hyster, and Crown forklifts operating in harsh cross-dock environments.

---

## 📱 Sourced Tablet Specification: HOTWAV R9 Pro 11"

* **Manufacturer:** HOTWAV
* **Model:** R9 Pro Rugged Android 14 Tablet
* **Display:** 11.0" 2K (1200 x 1920) High-Brightness Display (Outdoor Daylight Viewable, Glove Mode support).
* **Battery Capacity:** **20,080 mAh Mega-Battery**
  * *Critical Advantage:* Lasts 3 full 8-hour shifts on a single charge. Eliminates the need to splice expensive 48V-to-12V DC-to-DC electrical converters into the forklift wiring harness.
* **Durability Ratings:**
  * **IP68 & IP69K:** 100% dust-tight; resists high-pressure, high-temperature washdowns.
  * **MIL-STD-810H:** Drop-proof from 1.5 meters onto solid concrete.
  * **Operating Temperature:** -20°C to 60°C (-4°F to 140°F), perfect for cold-storage freezers.
* **Connectivity:** 4G LTE Dual SIM (built-in cellular backup if warehouse Wi-Fi drops) + Wi-Fi 5 + Bluetooth 5.0.
* **Cameras:** 64MP Rear Camera (sharp macro photos of Bill of Lading paperwork and shifted freight) + 16MP Front Camera.
* **Unit Cost:** ~$320.00 / tablet.

## 🦾 Mounting Blueprint: RAM MOUNTS Heavy-Duty System

To secure the tablet onto forklift roll-cage pillars without drilling or voiding equipment warranties:

1. **Base Clamp:** **RAM Tough-Claw™ Large Clamp Base** (RAP-400U or RAP-B-400U)
   * Clamps securely to round, square, or odd-shaped roll-cage bars (0.625" to 2.25" outer diameter).
2. **Articulating Arm:** **RAM Double Socket Arm (Medium / Long)**
   * Provides 360-degree rotation so the forklift operator can swing the tablet out of their line of sight while driving with forks forward.
3. **Cradle:** **RAM X-Grip® Universal 11"-12" Tablet Cradle** (RAM-HOL-UN11U)
   * Spring-loaded rubber-capped fingers grip the tablet tightly under extreme engine vibration.
* **Mounting Kit Cost:** ~$140.00 / kit.
