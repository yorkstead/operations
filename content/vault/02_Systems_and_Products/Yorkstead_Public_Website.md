# 🛠️ System Specification: Yorkstead Public Website

## 📌 Overview
The public face of Yorkstead Systems (`yorkstead.com`), communicating the company's distinct philosophy: *"Fixing the work before selling the software."*

* **Production URL:** [yorkstead.com](https://yorkstead.com)
* **Hosting:** Netlify
* **Stack:** Next.js, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Messaging & Conversions

* **Anti-SaaS Positioning:** Contrasts rigid $1,500/mo enterprise ERP subscriptions against focused, custom-owned tools.
* **Service Offerings:**
  * Internal operations software & shopfloor tools.
  * Custom customer & vendor portals.
  * Hardware & terminal integration (forklift tablets, barcode scanners).
  * High-performance business websites.
* **Lead Capture:** Direct inquiry funnel for Colorado manufacturing, fabrication, and logistics facilities.
