# 🛠️ System Specification: Barcodes & QR Tracking Utility

## 📌 Overview
A lightweight industrial utility for generating, rendering, and parsing Code 128 barcodes and high-density 2D DataMatrix/QR codes for warehouse inventory, pallet tags, and work-in-progress traveler sheets.

* **Codebase:** `c:\Users\4twen\dev\yorkstead-systems\demos\barcodes`
* **Features:**
  * High-speed batch SVG/Canvas barcode rendering.
  * Zebra ZPL print stream output for direct thermal label printers.
  * Honeywell / Zebra rugged scanner keyboard-wedge emulation parser.
