# 📞 Warm Referral Pitch: The Morelli Bridge Playbook

> **Target:** Michael Morelli & Rick Morelli  
> **Relationship Foundation:** Peer-to-peer connection from Yorkstead founder's former role as **Manufacturing Supervisor at Elward Systems**.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Objective
We do NOT ask the Morellis to buy software. We ask them for an operational sanity-check and an introduction to **Craig Bedore** (on-site dock operating principal) and **Steve Chapman / Dale Burget** (owners).

---

## 📱 Phone & Text Scripts

### 1. Opening Phone Conversation
> *"Hey Michael / Rick, this is [Your Name]. It's been a bit since our days coordinating panel loads and freight at Elward Systems! I wanted to reach out because I've been building custom operational systems tailored specifically for heavy transport and cross-docking along the I-70 corridor.*  
>  
> *I know how crazy things get when trucks come down Floyd Hill with shifted loads and blown-out pallets. I built a working terminal platform specifically around Denver Express's setup at 6030 Washington St—handles instant emergency driver holds, forklift damage photo capture on rugged tablets, and shuts down broker chargebacks.*  
>  
> *Before I sit down with Steve, Dale, or Craig, I wanted to buy you a cup of coffee or jump on a quick 5-minute call to get your take on it as a real freight operator. You guys know the day-to-day headaches better than anyone."*

### 2. Transitioning to Craig Bedore (On-Site Dock Principal)
> *"Craig, Michael and Rick mentioned I should talk directly to you because you're the one actually running the dock doors and dealing with the forklift crews when a trailer pulls in with tipped pallets.*  
>  
> *I came out of industrial manufacturing supervision over at Elward, so I know you don't need another tech kid trying to sell you monthly software rent. I built this so your guys on the Toyota lifts have rugged, glove-friendly tablets that take watermarked before/after photos and capture driver signatures on the glass, while the office gets an automatic chime when a truck reserves.*  
>  
> *Can I swing by 6030 Washington on Tuesday morning with the tablet mounted on a clamp to show you and the crew in person?"*
