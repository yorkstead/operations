# 💰 Commercial Strategy: The \$9,500 Capital Asset Buyout

> **The Yorkstead Philosophy:** Blue-collar industrial businesses hate monthly software subscriptions ("SaaS rent"). They invest in forklifts, real estate, and racking as balance-sheet capital assets. Software should be treated the same way.

---

## 🎯 The Commercial Offer

* **Total Software Purchase:** **\$9,500.00 (One-Time)**
  * **Payment Terms:** 50% upon signing (\$4,750.00), 50% upon on-site delivery and staff training (\$4,750.00).
* **What is Included:**
  * 100% full source code ownership (deposited to Client's GitHub).
  * **15-Mile Territorial Exclusivity Covenant** along the I-70/I-25 freight interchange.
  * **SpinFlow Handover Architecture** with 1-hour engineering transition session.
  * On-site terminal setup, bay calibration, and staff training at 6030 Washington St.
  * Zero per-user, per-forklift, or per-rework software licensing fees forever.

## 📈 Economic ROI & Payback Math

* **Average Emergency Rework Fee:** \$480 – \$650 per truck.
* **Volume Reality:** Several stranded OTR trucks bounce off Denver Express every week due to phone tag and lack of instant bay reservations.
* **The ROI Equation:** Capturing just **2 additional trucks per week** generates **\$4,160/month in net new revenue (\$49,920 annually)**.
* **Payback Period:** The entire \$9,500 investment pays for itself in less than **58 days**.
