## 🎯 Strategic Handover Positioning: "Ally, Not Competitor"
Sales deals with midsize businesses often collapse when the client's incumbent web agency tells the owner: *"Don't buy from them, we can build that for you."*

Yorkstead neutralizes this threat completely through the **Agency Handover Architecture**:
1. **Explicit Division of Territory:** Yorkstead makes clear we do not build marketing websites or provide SEO retainers. We build specialized industrial shopfloor and terminal systems.
2. **Clean Codebase Handover:** ReworkFlow is built using modern Next.js 15, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS — standard, widely loved tools in any web agency's wheelhouse.
3. **Agency Hosting & Custody:** Yorkstead offers SpinFlow the full repository so they can host the application on `rework.denverexpressco.com` and bill their own ongoing retainer to Denver Express.
4. **Result:** SpinFlow becomes a champion for the deal rather than an obstacle.
