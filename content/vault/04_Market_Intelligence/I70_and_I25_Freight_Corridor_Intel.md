# 🔍 Market Intelligence: I-70 & I-25 Freight Corridor Intelligence

## 📌 Geographic Leverage: The "Mousetrap"
Denver Express's facility at **6030 Washington St** sits precisely at the junction of Interstate 25 and Interstate 70, colloquially known as Colorado's "Mousetrap". This is the highest-volume freight convergence node in the Rocky Mountain West.

* **Floyd Hill Factor:** Westbound and eastbound freight navigating I-70 between Idaho Springs and Golden must negotiate severe 6% mountain grades and sharp curves. Heavy braking and sudden downshifts frequently snap pallet bands, collapse stretch wrap, and pitch 2,000 lb pallets into trailer sidewalls.
* **The Eisenhower Tunnel Chokepoint:** Overweight or shifted loads that cannot pass chain-law inspection points or scale houses (Dumount Weigh Station) are turned back toward Denver for mandatory re-blocking and bracing.
* **ELD (Electronic Logging Device) Time Pressures:** Long-haul drivers operating under strict FMCSA Hours of Service (11-hour driving / 14-hour on-duty window) who arrive at a consignee with a shifted load cannot afford to wait 4 hours for a dock bay. A 45-minute guaranteed hold is often the difference between making their delivery and a mandatory 10-hour sleeper berth penalty.

## 💳 Payment Realities: Comcheks & Fleet Codes
In 80% of emergency cross-dock scenarios, the stranded driver does not pay out of pocket:
* **Comchek / EFS / T-Chek:** The carrier's broker or fleet dispatch issues an express payment authorization code once an upfront cost estimate is confirmed.
* **The Paperwork Bottleneck:** Brokers will immediately freeze or withhold Comchek funds if the dock cannot provide clear before-and-after photos proving that the cargo was safely restacked and secured.
* **ReworkFlow Solution:** Automated generation of the signed Evidence Certificate PDF provides instant verifiable proof that brokers cannot reject.
