# 🔍 Market Intelligence: Competitor Landscape Teardown

## 📌 Primary Competitors in Regional Logistics

### 1. Traditional Warehouse ERPs (HighJump, Manhattan, Blue Yonder)
* **Target:** 500,000+ sq ft multinational distribution centers.
* **Flaws:** $50,000+ implementation costs, 6-month onboarding cycles, bloated menus, and unusable on a moving forklift.
* **Yorkstead Advantage:** 14-day deployment, zero bloat, 90-second mobile intake.

### 2. Emerging Freight Cross-Dock Software (FreightBox, FMG)
* **Business Model:** Aggressive per-load transaction fees (3%–5% cut) or $1,200/mo recurring SaaS.
* **Flaws:** Dock operators hate paying a slice of every load to an out-of-state software vendor.
* **Yorkstead Advantage:** $9,500 flat buyout with 100% code ownership. 0% transaction cut.

### 3. The Incumbent Champion: Paper Clipboards & Dry-Erase Boards
* **Usage:** 85% of regional cross-dock operations still run on physical paper and dry-erase marker boards.
* **Flaws:** Grease stains, water damage, lost receipts, broker disputes, zero searchability, and impossible for highway drivers to book online.
* **Yorkstead Advantage:** Operates as fast as paper, but produces instantaneous digital proof and billing records.
