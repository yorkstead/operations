# Client Onboarding

1. Qualify fit and complete a workflow audit.
2. Map current state, owners, systems of record, pain, volume, exceptions, and measurable baseline.
3. Define a narrow first release, exclusions, acceptance tests, security/data responsibilities, and success metrics.
4. Inventory data and integrations; obtain sample data through approved secure channels.
5. Configure a sandbox, map roles, migrate representative data, and validate with real scenarios.
6. Pilot with named champions, train by role, collect defects, and rehearse support/rollback.
7. Cut over with ownership, monitoring, backups, communication, and a defined hypercare period.
8. Review outcomes and decide the next bounded release.

Never promise savings or dates without explicit assumptions and evidence.
